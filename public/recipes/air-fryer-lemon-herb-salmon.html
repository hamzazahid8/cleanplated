Crispy Air Fryer Lemon Herb Salmon with Garlic Asparagus

Juicy, flaky salmon crusted in zesty lemon and herbs, paired with tender roasted asparagus in under 20 minutes.

Prep Time 8 mins
Cook Time 10 mins
Total Time 18 mins
Protein 44g
Calories 420 kcal
Servings 2

Why This Recipe Works

This Air Fryer Lemon Herb Salmon is our favorite weeknight lifesaver. The air fryer circulates 400°F convection heat to achieve a delicate golden crust on the salmon while locking in all the natural omega-3 moisture inside. Paired with crisp-tender garlic asparagus cooked simultaneously in the same basket, dinner is on the table in 18 minutes flat with virtually zero cleanup.

  • Convection air frying crisps the outside in 10 minutes without drying the center.
  • High-protein (44g) and omega-3 fatty acids promote satiety and heart health.
  • Zero stovetop splatter and single-basket cooking.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Prep the Salmon & Asparagus

Pat the salmon fillets completely dry with paper towels. Dry salmon guarantees a crisp, caramelized surface rather than steaming. Place trimmed asparagus alongside the salmon on a large cutting board.

2

Season and Coat

In a small ramekin, whisk together extra virgin olive oil, minced garlic, lemon zest, Italian herbs, smoked paprika, sea salt, and black pepper. Brush generously over both sides of the salmon and toss with the asparagus spears.

3

Air Fry to Perfection

Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for 3 minutes. Place salmon skin-side down in the center of the basket and arrange asparagus spears around the sides. Top salmon fillets with lemon slices.

4

Cook and Check Internal Temp

Air fry at 400°F for 9–11 minutes depending on thickness. Insert an instant-read meat thermometer into the thickest part; salmon is perfectly medium-rare and tender when it reaches 130°F to 135°F (57°C).

5

Rest and Garnish

Remove carefully with a silicone spatula, rest for 2 minutes, squeeze remaining fresh lemon juice over the top, and serve immediately.

💡 Chef's Secret Tips

  • Use an instant-read thermometer: Salmon cooks fast in an air fryer; 10 minutes at 400°F is usually the sweet spot for a 1-inch fillet.
  • Skin-on is best: The skin acts as a heat shield, preventing the bottom meat from overcooking while releasing healthy oils.
