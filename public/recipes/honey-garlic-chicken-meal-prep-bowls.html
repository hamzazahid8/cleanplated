20-Minute Honey Garlic Chicken Meal Prep Bowls with Fluffy Quinoa

Tender caramelized chicken bites glazed in a sweet-savory garlic sauce over fluffy quinoa and crisp broccoli.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 10 mins
Total Time 20 mins
Protein 42g
Calories 465 kcal
Servings 4

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Whisk the Glaze

In a small bowl, whisk together the honey, low-sodium soy sauce/aminos, grated garlic, and freshly grated ginger until smooth. Set aside.

2

Sear Chicken Bites

Heat a large non-stick skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat. Spray with avocado oil. Add diced chicken in a single layer and sear for 5–6 minutes, flipping halfway until golden brown on all sides and internal temp hits 165°F.

3

Glaze and Thicken

Pour the honey garlic sauce directly over the hot chicken. Cook for 60 to 90 seconds, stirring constantly. The sauce will bubble vigorously and reduce into a thick, glossy lacquer coating every chicken morsel.

4

Assemble Meal Prep Containers

Divide 3/4 cup cooked quinoa and 1 cup steamed broccoli evenly into 4 glass meal prep containers. Top with the glazed honey garlic chicken.

5

Garnish and Store

Sprinkle with toasted sesame seeds and sliced green onions. Let cool slightly before snapping airtight lids on. Store refrigerated for up to 5 days.

💡 Chef's Secret Tips

  • Do not crowd the skillet: If chicken is crowded, it will steam instead of caramelize. Cook in two batches if your pan is smaller than 12 inches.
  • Reheating tip: Sprinkle a teaspoon of water over the quinoa before microwaving for 90 seconds to restore fluffy moisture.
