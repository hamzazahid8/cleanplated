12-Minute Lemon Dill Garlic Butter Shrimp with Asparagus

Sweet, tender wild shrimp and fresh asparagus sautéed in golden garlic butter and lemon dill in 12 quick minutes.

Prep Time 5 mins
Cook Time 7 mins
Total Time 12 mins
Protein 39g
Calories 340 kcal
Servings 2

When you need dinner on the table in literally 12 minutes, this Lemon Dill Garlic Butter Shrimp is unmatched. Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Sauté Asparagus

Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat. Add cut asparagus pieces and a pinch of salt. Sauté for 3–4 minutes until bright green and crisp-tender. Push asparagus to one side of the pan.

2

Add Garlic & Sear Shrimp

Melt remaining 1 tbsp butter in the empty side of the skillet. Add minced garlic and red pepper flakes, cooking 30 seconds until fragrant. Add shrimp in a flat layer.

3

Cook Shrimp Rapidly

Cook shrimp for 2 minutes undisturbed, flip, and cook for another 1–2 minutes until opaque, pink, and curled into a loose 'C' shape.

4

Finish with Lemon & Fresh Dill

Toss shrimp and asparagus together. Remove skillet from heat immediately. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the pan and scatter fresh dill. Stir to coat in the pan juices.

💡 Chef's Secret Tips

  • Watch the shrimp shape: When shrimp curl into a 'C' shape, they are perfectly cooked and juicy. When they curl into a tight 'O', they are overcooked and rubbery.
  • Thaw frozen shrimp quickly: Place frozen shrimp in a bowl of cold running water for 8 minutes, then pat completely dry.
