Mediterranean Greek Salmon Power Bowl with Tzatziki & Warm Quinoa

Crispy seasoned salmon fillet over warm quinoa, Greek salad with kalamata olives, and cool homemade dill tzatziki.

Prep Time 10 mins
Cook Time 10 mins
Total Time 20 mins
Protein 42g
Calories 480 kcal
Servings 2

Why This Recipe Works

Ranked the #1 healthiest dietary pattern on the planet, Mediterranean eating comes alive in this vibrant Greek Salmon Power Bowl. Featuring herb-crusted pan-seared salmon nestled beside fluffy quinoa, diced Persian cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, pickled red onion, kalamata olives, and a dollop of high-protein Greek yogurt tzatziki. Every bite is crisp, refreshing, and deeply satisfying.

  • Combines healthy fats from wild salmon and Greek olive oil with antioxidants from colorful vegetables.
  • Tzatziki made with 0% Greek yogurt delivers creamy richness with an extra 8g of protein.
  • Versatile for weeknight dinners or grab-and-go cold lunches.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Season and Cook Salmon

Season salmon with oregano, garlic powder, sea salt, and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook salmon 4 minutes on the flesh side, flip and cook 3–4 minutes skin-side down until cooked through.

2

Make Quick Tzatziki

In a small bowl, stir Greek yogurt, grated cucumber, lemon juice, chopped dill, pinch of sea salt, and 1 minced garlic clove until combined.

3

Toss the Greek Salad

In a mixing bowl, toss cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, and kalamata olives with a splash of red wine vinegar and extra virgin olive oil.

4

Assemble the Bowls

Divide warm quinoa between two wide shallow bowls. Top with the seared salmon fillet and Greek salad. Add a generous dollop of tzatziki and sprinkle crumbled feta over the top.

💡 Chef's Secret Tips

  • Meal prep friendly: You can eat this bowl warm or cold straight out of the fridge on hot summer days.
  • Drain cucumber for tzatziki: Squeeze grated cucumber in a clean kitchen towel before adding to yogurt to keep the sauce thick and velvety.
