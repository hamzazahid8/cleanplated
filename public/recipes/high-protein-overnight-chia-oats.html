5-Minute High-Protein Blueberry Overnight Chia Oats

Creamy, naturally sweet overnight oats powered by organic chia seeds and vanilla whey for 35g of morning protein.

Prep Time 5 mins
Cook Time 0 mins

Why This Recipe Works

Say goodbye to chaotic weekday mornings. These High-Protein Blueberry Overnight Chia Oats take literally 5 minutes of prep before bed. Rolled oats and organic chia seeds soak overnight in almond milk and vanilla protein powder, absorbing the liquid to create a thick, pudding-like consistency. Packed with 35g of protein, 9g of gut-friendly fiber, and omega-3s to keep you energized until lunchtime.

  • Zero cooking required: The refrigerator does 100% of the work while you sleep.
  • Chia seeds expand up to 10x their weight in liquid, creating a luxurious spoonable texture.
  • Slow-digesting beta-glucan fiber prevents 10 AM energy crashes.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1

Combine Dry Ingredients

In a medium mixing bowl or two mason jars, combine rolled oats, organic chia seeds, vanilla protein powder, and a tiny pinch of sea salt. Whisk dry ingredients so the protein powder distributes evenly.

2

Whisk in Liquids

Pour in unsweetened almond milk, Greek yogurt, and vanilla extract. Whisk vigorously for 30 seconds until completely smooth with no protein powder clumps.

3

Layer and Refrigerate

Divide into two airtight glass meal prep jars. Top generously with fresh blueberries. Seal with lids and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or ideally overnight (up to 4 days).

4

Grab and Go

In the morning, give it a quick stir. Add a splash of cold almond milk if you prefer a thinner consistency, and enjoy cold straight from the jar!

💡 Chef's Secret Tips

  • Protein tip: Mix the protein powder with the milk before adding oats to ensure a velvety smooth texture with zero lumps.
  • Topping variations: Add a tablespoon of almond butter, crushed walnuts, or hemp hearts right before serving for extra healthy fats.
